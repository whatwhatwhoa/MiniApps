## V-M3 — Readings on the chart (2026-11-07)
- [ ] Now: under the big number, "+12.40 W  10 s average" (or −, orange on battery); it moves
      less than the big number.
- [ ] Settings → Average under the big number → 1 min: the label says "1 min average".
- [ ] Now's chart: tap it: a line, a dot and a bubble with the time (seconds) and watts; the
      line under the chart gives power, current, voltage and level; Clear removes it.
- [ ] Drag sideways along it: the bubble follows the finger; a drag up or down scrolls the page.
- [ ] Leave the cursor: it slides left with the chart and goes after 10 minutes.
- [ ] History → 24 h: touch the power chart: the level chart shows the cursor too; the line says
      "average of 4 m".
- [ ] A stretch with nothing logged: the bubble says "not logged".
